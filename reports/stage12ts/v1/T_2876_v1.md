The resulting shape is described as a **trapezoid**, and we are to find its **area**.

### Step 1: Understand the Geometry

Let’s define the rectangle with corners at:
- Bottom-left: $ A = (0, 0) $
- Bottom-right: $ B = (8, 0) $
- Top-left: $ D = (0, 5) $

- From $ (8,0) $ to $ (3,5) $, i.e., line $ y = -x + 8 $

### Step 2: Determine the Resulting Shape

After folding, the part of the paper that remains visible is the region **below both crease lines** within the original rectangle. This region forms a **trapezoid** with the following vertices:

- Bottom-left: $ A = (0, 0) $

- Top-right: $ (5, 5) $

- **Height**: vertical distance between the top and bottom bases = 5
