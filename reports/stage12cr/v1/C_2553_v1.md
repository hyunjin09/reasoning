A circle of diameter $1$ is removed from a $2\times 3$ rectangle, as shown.  Which whole number is closest to the area of the shaded region?

## Full raw response

We are given a rectangle of dimensions $2 \times 3$, and a circle of diameter $1$ is removed from it. We need to find the area of the **shaded region** (i.e., the area of the rectangle minus the area of the circle), and determine which whole number it is closest to.

---

### Step 1: Calculate the area of the rectangle

The rectangle has dimensions $2$ (height) by $3$ (width).

$$
\text{Area}_{\text{rectangle}} = 2 \times 3 = 6
$$

---

### Step 2: Calculate the area of the circle

The circle has **diameter 1**, so its **radius is $r = \frac{1}{2}$**.

The area of a circle is:

$$
\text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = \pi r^2 = \pi \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \pi \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{\pi}{4}
$$

Using $\pi \approx 3.1416$, we get:

$$
\frac{\pi}{4} \approx \frac{3.1416}{4} \approx 0.7854
$$

---

### Step 3: Calculate the shaded area

$$
\text{Shaded Area} = \text{Area}_{\text{rectangle}} - \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = 6 - 0.7854 \approx 5.2146
$$

---

### Step 4: Find the closest whole number

$5.2146$ is closer to **5** than to 6.

---

### ✅ Final Answer: **E. 5**
