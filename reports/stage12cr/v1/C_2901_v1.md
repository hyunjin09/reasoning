In the diagram, what is the perimeter of the sector of the circle with radius 12? Let's think step by step.

## Full raw response

We are given a sector of a circle with center O, radius 12, and angle ∠AOB = 60°.

The perimeter of a sector consists of:
1. The two straight radii (OA and OB)
2. The curved arc AB

Since the radius is 12, the length of each radius is 12.

So, the total length of the two radii is:  
**12 + 12 = 24**

Now, we need the length of the arc AB.

The formula for the arc length is:  
**Arc length = (θ / 360°) × 2πr**,  
where θ is the central angle in degrees and r is the radius.

Here, θ = 60°, r = 12.

So, arc length = (60 / 360) × 2π × 12  
= (1/6) × 24π  
= **4π**

Therefore, the total perimeter of the sector is:  
**Radii + Arc = 24 + 4π**

**Final Answer:**  
\boxed{24 + 4\pi}
