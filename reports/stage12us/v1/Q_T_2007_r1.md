A **regular hexagon** has:

- **One regular hexagon**,
- **Six triangles**,
- **Six squares**.

This implies that **each side of the hexagon** is associated with **one square** and **one triangle**, **on opposite sides** of the side. That is:
- One **square** is attached to one side of the hexagon (outside the hexagon),

This configuration ensures no overlap between the square and triangle along any shared edge, and both contribute **distinct outer edges** to the perimeter.

#### ✅ Triangle Contribution

#### ✅ Hexagon Contribution
All six sides of the hexagon are shared with either a square or a triangle, so they are **not part of the outer perimeter**.
