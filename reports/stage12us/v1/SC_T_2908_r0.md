  - **Arc segments**: Parts of the circles not covered by the straight segments.

### Step 2: Calculate the Straight Segments
- For any two circles of equal radius $r$, the length of the common external tangent is equal to the distance between centers, which is $2r$.
- Here, $r = 10$ cm, so the length of each straight segment is $20$ cm.
- There are 3 such segments (one between each pair of circles).
- Total straight segment length = $3 \times 20 = 60$ cm.

### Step 3: Calculate the Arc Segments
- At each circle, the belt is tangent to the circle, and the two straight segments meet at an angle of $120^\circ$ (since the triangle formed by the centers has angles of $60^\circ$ at each vertex, and the angle between the tangents is supplementary to this).
- The central angle for the arc segment on each circle is $180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ$.
- Convert $60^\circ$ to radians: $60^\circ = \frac{\pi}{3}$ radians.
- Arc length for one circle = $r \times \text{central angle} = 10 \times \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{10\pi}{3}$ cm.
- Total arc length for three circles = $3 \times \frac{10\pi}{3} = 10\pi$ cm.

### Step 4: Total Belt Length
- Total belt length = straight segments + arc segments = $60 + 10\pi$ cm.
- Here, $a = 60$ and $b = 10$.

### Step 5: Compute $a + b$
- $a + b = 60 + 10 = 70$.

The value of $a + b$ is 70.
