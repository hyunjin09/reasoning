- The **inner square** has side length $ s $,
- The **outer square** (including the wall) has side length $ s + 2 $,
- The **number of wall cubes** is the difference between the total number of cubes in the outer square and the inner square.

- $ s $ = side length of the **inner square** (in cubes),
