### **Setting the Areas Equal**

We set the two areas equal:
$$
\frac{1}{2}(\theta - \sin\theta) = \frac{1}{2} \tan\theta (1 - \cos\theta)
$$

Multiply both sides by 2:
$$
\theta - \sin\theta = \tan\theta (1 - \cos\theta)
$$

Now simplify the right-hand side using trigonometric identities:
$$
\tan\theta (1 - \cos\theta) = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}(1 - \cos\theta) = \sin\theta \left( \frac{1 - \cos\theta}{\cos\theta} \right)
$$

So,
$$
\theta - \sin\theta = \frac{\sin\theta (1 - \cos\theta)}{\cos\theta}
$$

Multiply both sides by $ \cos\theta $:

\cos\theta (\theta - \sin\theta) = \sin\theta (1 - \cos\theta)

- Left: $ \theta \cos\theta - \sin\theta \cos\theta $
- Right: $ \sin\theta - \sin\theta \cos\theta $
