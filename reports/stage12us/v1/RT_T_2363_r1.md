- A circle with center $ C $, radius 1 (for simplicity).
- $ AB $ is tangent to the circle at point $ A $.
- Line segments $ BCD $ and $ ACE $ intersect at $ C $, forming a straight line from $ B $ through $ C $ to $ D $, and another from $ A $ through $ C $ to $ E $, which is a vertical diameter.
- The angle $ \theta $ is measured at point $ C $ between the vertical line $ CE $ and the segment $ CD $, so the central angle $ \angle ECD = \theta $.

### Step 1: Identify the Two Shaded Areas

From the diagram description and standard geometric interpretations in such problems:

- **Small shaded area**: This is the **circular sector** $ ECD $, bounded by the radii $ CE $ and $ CD $, and the arc $ ED $. Since the central angle is $ \theta $ and the radius is 1, its area is:
  $$
  \text{Area}_{\text{sector}} = \frac{1}{2} \theta
  $$

- **Large shaded area**: This is the region bounded by:
  - Tangent $ AB $,
  - Secant $ BF $ (from point $ B $ to point $ F $, the intersection of line $ BC $ with the circle on the lower half),
  - Arc $ AF $ (from $ F $ back to $ A $ along the circle).

This region lies **inside triangle $ ABC $** but **outside the circle**, i.e., it's the part of triangle $ ABC $ that is **not covered by the circular segment $ AF $**. So its area is:
$$
\text{Area}_{\text{large}} = \text{Area}_{ABC} - \text{Area}_{\text{segment } AF}
$$

We compute each component:

- Triangle $ ABC $ is right-angled at $ A $, with legs $ AB = \tan \theta $ and $ AC = 1 $. Thus,
  $$
  \text{Area}_{ABC} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \tan \theta
  $$

- Segment $ AF $ corresponds to a sector $ CAF $ with angle $ \theta $ (since angle $ \angle ACF = \theta $), so:
  $$
  \text{Area}_{\text{sector } CAF} = \frac{1}{2} \theta,\quad \text{Area}_{\triangle CAF} = \frac{1}{2} \sin \theta
  $$
  $$
  \text{Area}_{\text{segment } AF} = \frac{1}{2} \theta - \frac{1}{2} \sin \theta
  $$

Thus, the large shaded area becomes:
$$
\text{Area}_{\text{large}} = \frac{1}{2} \tan \theta - \left( \frac{1}{2} \theta - \frac{1}{2} \sin \theta \right) = \frac{1}{2} (\tan \theta - \theta + \sin \theta)

$$
\tan \theta (1 - \cos \theta) + \sin \theta = \tan \theta

\text{Area}_{\text{large}} = \frac{1}{2} (\tan \theta - \theta)
