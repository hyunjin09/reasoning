- $ TB $: intersects the circle at $ D $ (closer to $ T $) and $ B $ (farther from $ T $)

$$
\angle RTB = \frac{1}{2} (\text{arc } BR - \text{arc } DS)

$$
28 = \frac{1}{2} (\text{arc } BR - \text{arc } DS) \Rightarrow \text{arc } BR - \text{arc } DS = 56^\circ \tag{1}
$$

#### **2. Central Angles and Arc Measures**

Since both angles $ \angle ROB $ and $ \angle SOT $ are **central angles**, their measures are equal to the measures of the arcs they intercept:
