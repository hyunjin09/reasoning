  - Therefore, **two shared edges** per hexagon side: one between hexagon and square, and one between square and triangle.

This configuration ensures that **each shape is only connected to others at the internal edges**, and the **outer perimeter** consists entirely of the **unshared edges** of the hexagon, squares, and triangles.
