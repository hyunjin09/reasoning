### **Step 1: Straight Segments of the Belt**

Each straight segment of the belt corresponds to the external tangent between two adjacent circles. For two circles of equal radius $r = 10$, separated by a distance $d = 20$ cm (since they are tangent), the length of the external tangent is:

$$
L_{\text{straight}} = d = 20 \text{ cm}
$$

There are **three** such straight segments (one between each pair of circles), so the total length contributed by the straight segments is:

$$
3 \times 20 = 60 \text{ cm}
$$

---

### **Step 2: Arc Segments of the Belt**

The belt also curves around each circle. At each point of tangency, the belt forms a smooth arc on the circle. To find the angle of the arc on each circle, consider the triangle formed by the centers of the three circles — an equilateral triangle with side length 20 cm.

$$

$$

$$

$$

$$
3 \times \frac{20\pi}{3} = 20\pi \text{ cm}
$$
