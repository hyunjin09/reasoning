---

### Step 1: Area of the rectangle

The rectangle has dimensions 2 units by 3 units.

$$
\text{Area}_{\text{rectangle}} = 2 \times 3 = 6
$$

---

### Step 2: Area of the circle

$$
\text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = \pi r^2 = \pi \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \pi \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{\pi}{4}
$$

---

### Step 3: Area of the shaded region

$$
\text{Shaded Area} = 6 - \frac{\pi}{4}

$$
\frac{\pi}{4} \approx \frac{3.1416}{4} \approx 0.7854
$$

$$
\text{Shaded Area} \approx 6 - 0.7854 = 5.2146
$$

---

5.2146 is **closer to 5** than to 6.
