- Radius $ r = 12 $
- Central angle $ \theta = 60^\circ $

The **perimeter** of a sector consists of:

1. The two **radii** (each of length $ r $)
2. The **arc length** between the two radii

---

### Step 1: Calculate the arc length

The formula for arc length is:

$$
\text{Arc length} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi r
$$

Plug in $ \theta = 60^\circ $ and $ r = 12 $:

$$
\text{Arc length} = \frac{60}{360} \times 2\pi \times 12 = \frac{1}{6} \times 24\pi = 4\pi
$$

---

### Step 2: Add the two radii

Each radius is 12, so total length of radii = $ 12 + 12 = 24 $

---

### Step 3: Total perimeter

$$
\text{Perimeter} = \text{Arc length} + \text{Two radii} = 4\pi + 24
$$

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

\boxed{24 + 4\pi}
$$
