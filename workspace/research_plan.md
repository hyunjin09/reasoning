## Objective

Determine when a Thinking MLLM causally depends on fresh visual evidence, what evidence it uses, what drives transitions in visual access, how acquired evidence supports later reasoning, and how these dynamics differ on incorrect trajectories.

## Primary Scope

- Primary model: Qwen3-VL-4B-Thinking.
- Primary benchmark: MathVerse.
- Initial scale: instrumentation validation on approximately 30–50 examples.
- Study type: analysis and mechanistic interpretability.
- Measurement principle: begin with continuous causal quantities, not raw-attention thresholds or discrete LOOK/THINK labels.

## Program Sequence

1. Instrumentation.
2. Baseline dynamics.
3. Paired MathVerse modality conditions.
4. Evidence-specific causal usage.
5. Tests of why visual-access transitions occur.
6. Handoff and carrier mechanisms.
7. Evidence lifecycle.
8. Failure analysis.

Only after this evidence program should the project decide whether a new intervention or method is justified.

## Fixed Methodological Boundaries

- Do not equate attention magnitude with causal usage.
- Do not infer visual memory from low direct-vision dependence alone.
- Separate explicit chain-of-thought memory from latent hidden/KV memory.
- Separate local teacher-forced effects from downstream free-rollout effects.
- Use matched controls for causal image or ROI interventions.
- Do not force the LOOK/THINK hypothesis if observations support a different mechanism.
- Do not begin by designing re-grounding, routing, pruning, or attention-control methods.
