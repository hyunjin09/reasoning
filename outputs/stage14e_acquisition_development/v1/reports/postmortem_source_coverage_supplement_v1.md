# Completion of source review across all 72 SAVA families

The preserved SAVA audit supplied structured helper-source reviews for 52 of the 72 families, including the frozen 24-family acquisition panel. The remaining 20 were reviewed retrospectively during the new cycle. The supplement adds 100 logical G/Q/QI/QIA/QGL records, including aliases and absent helpers, without changing any old annotation or headline estimate. Exact source images, reference atoms, prefix hashes, helper outputs and generation hashes are in `annotations/postmortem_remaining20_source_review_v1.json`.

This is a single-AI, condition-visible development review. Historical final outcomes were already observed, so it is not outcome-blind confirmation. New method-evaluation outcomes do not yet exist. These extra reviews did not revise the already frozen ledger method or its selection rule.

In the additional 20 families, QI supplied three partial correct reinspections (32, 1080, 1806), nine correct but already available facts, one explicit wrong read (120), four unknown/unhelpful records, and three absent helpers. None supplied a clearly new task-critical fact under the new review rubric. The partial reinspections do not certify complete source acquisition: 32 returns the correct containment count without naming the containing circles; 1080 confirms one step label without the rearrangement correspondence; 1806 returns a triangular shape without the shaded-flap/overlap binding. This rubric is not silently pooled with the earlier audit's differently worded novelty labels.

The new source comparisons reinforce specific obstacles:

- **Source relationships are lost even when the returned symbol is correct.** 968 returns the known character `0`, omitting its cube adjacency; 1120 repeats the correctly known `4`, omitting the misplaced `6`; 1722 returns two squares instead of the requested circles and never recovers addition alignment.
- **A tentative wrong premise may be repeated across readers.** 120's helpers return heights 12 and 4 while the image gives 11 and 6. This is consistent with prefix influence but does not independently prove its causal role.
- **Localization alone is insufficient.** 2547's targeted readers call an interior top-edge notch an end notch, including the global-plus-local arm.
- **Some missing information is a binding, not another number.** 1772 needs branch merges and outlet-to-container relationships; an unqualified fork count is insufficient. 2360 needs block orientations and arrow endpoints, not another copy of the given `32`.
- **Appropriate abstention exists.** 2748 already states every bar height correctly; the remaining operation is arithmetic. Its native-alias fallback is not an acquisition failure.

All 72 families now have a source-linked query/address/prefix analysis and helper-source review coverage (52 preserved, 20 supplementary). Missing/ambiguous readouts remain unknown. The frozen 24-family panel remains the denominator for the original SAVA acquisition estimate, and the new 24-family diagnostic remains a separate prospective development experiment.
